# Predictions

## November 2nd, 2026:

My ultimate prediction is that the geometry of neural networks, and the dynamics of the residual streams themselves provide understudied avenues to understand the psychology of LLMs and AI.  SAEs have done well in tracing which circuits light up for which concepts, and have produces interesting results in documenting vector representations in the networks.  However, decision making, reasoning, belief, psychology are described by a more complex landscapes.  The existing work here is simple; using toy models to study the changes in information content for opposing pairs of prompts.  I am compute limited, but I wish to apply similar concepts to intelligent prompts.
